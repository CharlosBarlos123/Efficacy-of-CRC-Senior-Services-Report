This project is the result of a freelance analysis that I had done for a not-profit in Columbus Ohio called the Clintonville-Beechwold Resource Center. This organization
helps seniors and impoverished individuals in the locality. I had the honor of producing a report based on data supplied to me that measures the efficacy of a senior
service that is designed to stave off the effects of old age. I produced statistically significant results with a paired student's T-test to determine that such an
initiative is worth additional and continued funding.
